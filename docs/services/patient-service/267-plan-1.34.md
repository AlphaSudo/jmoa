# Patient Plan 1.34: qualify a clean, scoped 8 MiB RAM result

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: **PLANNED_FOR_REVIEW; no new experiment authorized or launched by writing this plan.**

Predecessor: [JMOA 2.15](https://github.com/AlphaSudo/jmoa/releases/tag/v2.15), published as provisional benchmark evidence. Plan 1.33 is complete and immutable. This successor has its own [fixed 32-item checklist](268-checklist-1.34.md) and machine-readable contract in `267-plan-1.34.json`. It does not reopen, enlarge, or relabel the old checklist.

## 1. Objective and meaning of clean

Establish a **fresh direct reduction of at least 8 MiB in median Patient-process PSS**, with a one-sided lower saving bound at least 8 MiB, passing same-artifact calibration **without a precision exception**, exact public source-to-tested-artifact lineage, and qualified costs under declared practical bounds.

The intended statement is:

> In the identified Patient deployment, a fresh paired experiment measured at least 8 MiB lower median process PSS after the defined workload, with passing calibration and the disclosed, qualified startup, CPU and request-cost bounds.

The statement must still identify its Java/Leyden, packaging, workload, cache and host conditions. Clean means that the declared gates passed; it does not mean zero performance overhead, universal savings, proof without statistical assumptions, cold-start capacity, whole-stack savings, or validation of an untested live-ML deployment.

The **8 MiB floor is 8,192 KiB**. Add a prospective **128 KiB materiality guard**: the raw lower saving bound must reach **8,320 KiB (8.125 MiB)**, leaving128 KiB of fixed headroom above8 MiB. This guard is a design allowance, not a bias correction or a new confidence bound. The human previously accepted8.11 MiB; report that comparison too, but do not round a value below8 MiB into a win. No addition of historical medians, subtraction of an estimated bias to manufacture acceptance, post-observation precision exception, relaxed final threshold, or favorable reroll is permitted.

### E. Fresh precision and cost calibration — eight B0 units

Memory admission requires all three unchanged limits:

- Maximum absolute within-pair null PSS difference **≤512 KiB**.
- Absolute median null bias **≤128 KiB**.
- Absolute baseline drift **≤1,024 KiB**.

Cost admission retains all 82 metrics, raw response oracles and the fixed stationarity rule. Deliverables: `null-calibration.json`, all eight raw gates, and a single admission decision. Confirmation cannot start until memory and cost admission pass.

- Repeated delete: preserve create/delete grouping, transaction/oracle and data-size semantics; inspect GC, database/index, client and server timing before changing code.

A real candidate regression is a product issue. Fix it symmetrically when it is an application defect, or in the optimizer when it is an optimizer defect; then rebuild/retrain/requalify affected material. Unsupported explanations remain hypotheses. No endpoint deletion, disabled instrumentation feature, lowered functionality or hidden timeout can earn clean acceptance.

## 8. Technical references checked for this plan

- [Java 26 launcher and AOT cache contract](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/26/docs/specs/man/java.html): cache identity depends on application/JDK/OS/architecture; `AOTCache` and `AOTCacheOutput` cannot be combined. Use the actual pinned runtime's supported flags and fail on fallback. This prevents repeating the old incompatible-OFF-flags mistake.
- [Linux cgroup v2](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html): record event deltas at the target and ancestors; hierarchical and local event files have different scopes. Process PSS, cgroup total and file charges are distinct accounting quantities.
- [Linux PSI](https://docs.kernel.org/accounting/psi.html): recent averages and cumulative stall totals answer different questions. Use timestamped interval deltas to detect pressure bursts rather than relying on a single avg10 snapshot.
