# Patient V1 CDS Historical Audit

**Historical experiment record.** Measurements and dispositions below belong
to their original artifact/runtime protocol. Current publication boundaries are
[stable 2.1 and provisional 2.15](../releases/current-state.md); older medians are not added to those
results and older verdicts do not replace them.

Classification: `V1_CDS_HISTORICAL_INCOMPLETE`.

Phase 31 contains six accepted runs and three optimized-versus-baseline pairs. The optimized candidate won by approximately `4.2 MB PSS`, `4.3 MB Private_Dirty`, and `4.4 MB memory.current`; every run completed 600 requests without errors. Separate baseline and optimized CDS archives are recoverable and hashed.

The evidence does not freeze the exact historical artifact SHA/source revision and does not compare one V1 artifact under no-CDS and CDS. It therefore supports the original candidate claim, but not the narrower claim that CDS itself benefited V1.

The current fixed-artifact V1 policy screen supplies that missing comparator.
