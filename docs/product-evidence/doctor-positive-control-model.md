# Doctor Positive-Control Model

**Historical experiment record.** Measurements and dispositions below belong
to their original artifact/runtime protocol. Current publication boundaries are
[stable 2.1 and provisional 2.15](../releases/current-state.md); older medians are not added to those
results and older verdicts do not replace them.

Doctor is the complete-product positive control for `B0 -> V2`; it is not evidence that `B0 -> V1` wins independently.

- B0 -> V1 median PSS: 654 KB.
- B0 -> V2 median PSS: -4715.5 KB; wins: 5/6.
- Runtime: SPRING_BOOT_FAT_JAR / APPLICATION_CDS.
- Causal reading: V1 overhead is low-signal, while V1 -> V2 is large enough to produce the complete product win.
